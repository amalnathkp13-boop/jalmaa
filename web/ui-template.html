Model: coaxial loop coupling with matched resonant coils (Q limited to f / 2R by the data rate), conduction loss from the water skin depth, 2 cm of tissue at 0.5 S/m, receiver noise kTB + NF + man-made noise with the ITU-R P.372 slope of −27.7 dB per decade, non-coherent BFSK needing Eb/N0 ≈ 13.4 dB for BER 10⁻⁵. It is a first-order design model; the bucket test sets the real numbers.

What the simulation cannot prove

The physiology is synthetic and the link model is first order. Before the Stage-2 abstract, measure: margin versus distance and angle with the real coils in a tub of warm tap water (with 0.5 S/m saline as the stress case), electrode impedance under the sealed skirt during 2 h immersion, Doppler FHR against a commercial monitor on a phantom, and ZHF temperature against tympanic readings. Put the measured coil Q, noise floor and path loss into the Link budget tab and re-run the validation suite.
